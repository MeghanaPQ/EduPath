# EduPath AI — 5-Minute Demo Script

**Demo login:** `alex@edupath.demo` / `demo1234`  
**Banner:** DEMO MODE

## Scene 1 — Dashboard

Open `/dashboard`.

Show:

- Greeting for Alex
- Agent Status: ACTIVE
- Last scan / next scan
- Opportunity and application stats

## Scene 2 — Find Opportunities

Click **Find Opportunities**.

Watch Agent Activity progress:

- Searching trusted sources
- Extracting
- Evaluating
- Ranking

## Scene 3 — Results

Show summary counts:

- Opportunities discovered
- Eligible / strong matches
- High-priority items

Open `/opportunities` and sort by match.

## Scene 4 — Top Opportunity

Open **AI Research Fellowship**.

Highlight:

- Match ~90%+
- Why you match (GPA, degree, field, interest)
- Missing: Statement of Purpose

## Scene 5 — Application Readiness

Show readiness percentage and document checklist.

## Scene 6 — Resume / SOP Analyzer

Paste or use seeded resume text.

Show alignment dimensions and advisory suggestions.

Generate an SOP draft and point out the **AI-GENERATED — REVIEW** mark.

## Scene 7 — Calendar

Open `/calendar` and highlight upcoming deadlines.

## Scene 8 — Simulated Autonomous Scan

On Agent Activity or Dashboard, run discovery with **simulate new opportunity**.

Show a newly discovered fellowship appear.

## Scene 9 — Notification

Open notification center:

- NEW OPPORTUNITY FOUND
- Match score and deadline

## Scene 10 — Application Tracker

Open `/applications` and an in-progress item.

Show timeline:

`Submitted → Under Review → Interview`

---

Close with:

> **EduPath searches for the student, instead of making the student search.**
